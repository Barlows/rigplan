Free planning tools

Electrical calculators for mobile rigs.

These use the same sizing engine as the diagram editor. Pick a standard, enter real numbers, and read the result with its reasoning — no sign-up and nothing sent anywhere.

Cable size Fuse size AWG ↔ mm² Battery bank Energy budget Solar & MPPT Inverter DC-DC charger Shore charging State of charge

Cable size

Ampacity and voltage drop against the selected standard. The smallest passing size wins.

Voltage drop is calculated on the round trip (2 × length). Ambient, grouping and method derate the published ampacity before the comparison.

Fuse size

The next standard rating above the continuous design current, inside a family that suits the job.

AWG ↔ mm²

Convert between imperial and metric conductor sizes, with the nearest standard size.

Battery bank

Series and parallel layout, total capacity and usable energy for a chosen depth of discharge.

Energy budget

Add the loads you expect to run, then size the bank and the solar array around them.

Solar array & MPPT

Enter the panel nameplate, then read the array totals, controller current and cold-weather voltage check.

Inverter DC feed

Battery current, cable size and fuse for an inverter at a known AC load.

DC-DC charger

Input current, cable and fusing on both sides of a battery-to-battery charger.

Shore charging

DC charge current, AC draw and how long a bank takes to recharge from a given charger.

State of charge

Resting-voltage estimate for a 12 V pack. Give the battery half an hour of rest for a useful reading.

These calculators are planning aids. Published tables are simplified to a single installation method and a conservative set of derating factors. Check the final design against the current edition of the standard and the equipment manufacturer's instructions.
